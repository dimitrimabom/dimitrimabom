# 👋 Hi, I'm Dimitri Mabom

### 🚀 Front-End Developer | Tech Enthusiast | Visual Identity Lover

Welcome to my GitHub! Dive into my world of code, design, and creativity. Here, you'll find a collection of my front-end projects, tools, and experiments.

## 🎯 About Me

I'm a passionate **front-end developer** with a strong focus on **React.js** and **Next.js**. I hold a professional degree in software engineering and have a creative eye for logos and visual identities.

Currently, I work at **AFRO Engineering Cameroon** as a **Front Office Engineer Team Leader for MTN Cameroon**, where I blend my expertise in telecommunications and development.

When I'm not coding, you'll find me watching the latest **anime**, reading **manga**, or immersing myself in **sci-fi** movies. I also love exchanging ideas about the newest technologies and pop culture trends.

## ✨ What I Do

* 💻 **Front-End Development**: I craft responsive, accessible, and high-performance web interfaces with attention to UX.
* 📚 **Content Creation**: I run a YouTube channel where I mix tech tutorials with anime and sci-fi culture.
